# Terms of Use for Restricted Data

These terms apply to every dataset of the **BERA Human and Wildlife Team** marked 🔒 *On request* or *Internal*, which are held in `BERA-Human-and-Wildlife-Restricted-Data`. They do **not** apply to datasets marked ✅ *Open*; those are covered by the licence in their own folder.

Restricted datasets are **not** released under an open licence. **All rights are reserved by the data owner** named in each dataset's README. Access is granted to a specific person, for a specific purpose, on the conditions below.

## Conditions

By accepting access, you agree to the following:

1. **Approved purpose only.** Use the data only for the purpose described in your approved request. Contact the data owner before using it for anything else.
2. **No redistribution.** Do not share, post, publish or pass the data, in whole or in part, to anyone else, including colleagues and students. This also covers derived datasets from which the original data could be reconstructed. Anyone else who needs the data must make their own request.
3. **Non-commercial use only.** Do not use the data for commercial purposes, or on behalf of a commercial organization, without written permission from the data owner.
4. **Protect sensitive information.** Keep the data secure. Do not publish exact site coordinates or any personal information without the data owner's agreement.
5. **Credit and co-authorship.** Cite the dataset as given in its README, or the associated publication once available, and acknowledge BERA – Boreal Ecosystem Recovery and Assessment. Before submitting any publication, report or presentation that uses the data, contact the data owner to discuss co-authorship.
6. **No warranty.** The data are provided as is. The data owner and BERA are not responsible for any errors in the data or for how they are used.
7. **End of access.** Access may be withdrawn at any time. When your approved project ends or access is withdrawn, stop using the data and delete any copies, unless the data owner agrees otherwise.

## Accepting these terms

Before you are given access, you will be asked to confirm by email that you have read and accept these terms. Reply to the approval email with: *"I have read and agree to the BERA Human and Wildlife Team Terms of Use for Restricted Data."*

## When data become open

When a study is published, its dataset moves to the public repository under an open licence, and these terms stop applying to it.
